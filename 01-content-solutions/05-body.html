Persona-powered content development

What it means to run content off one shared brain

Most marketing teams don't have a content problem — they have a coordination problem. The SEO tool doesn't know what the email platform learned. The ad account has never seen the return-rate signal in the CRM. Every tool holds a partial picture of the customer, and a person becomes the integration layer, re-exporting segments and re-writing briefs to keep them roughly in sync. The result is content that's technically on-brand but aimed at nobody in particular.

Triangul8's solutions solve the coordination problem by design. Persona Engine builds the personas once, from your real first-party data, and every other module — SEO/AEO/GEO, website content, social, paid media, email, and marketing automation — reads from that same source of truth. A fact learned in one channel is instantly available in all of them. That's what “one shared brain” actually delivers: not a suite of tools that happen to share a login, but a system where knowing the audience compounds across every piece of content you publish.

How each solution uses the persona

Every module below starts from the same verified persona and scores its output on a real business metric — never vanity engagement.

Persona Engine

Clusters your customers into statistically distinct, addressable personas — as many as your data supports, not a fixed three or five. Each persona is sized, scored for intent and propensity, and refreshed automatically as your customer base shifts. This is the foundation every other solution reads from.

SEO / AEO / GEO

Builds content that ranks in traditional search and earns citations in AI answer engines like ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, and Google's AI Overviews. One page serves classic SEO and generative-engine optimization at once, structured around the questions your personas actually ask, and tracked for share of voice across engines.

Website Content

Generates landing pages, category pages, and product detail copy written for the specific persona most likely to arrive on each one — drafted straight into your CMS. Instead of one generic voice across the whole catalog, each page speaks to the segment behind the traffic.

Social — Paid & Organic

Produces persona-specific copy and creative briefs for organic feeds and paid social alike, tuned to each persona's channel affinity and scored on cost per acquisition rather than likes. The same personas keep messaging consistent from feed to funnel.

Paid Media

Writes search and programmatic ad copy per persona before budget goes out the door, scored against the metric each channel is judged on — conversion rate and cost per acquisition, never impressions. Spend follows the personas most likely to convert.

Email

Drafts subject lines, flows, and sends per persona, scored on revenue per send instead of open rate, and shipped to your ESP as drafts. Lifecycle messaging finally reflects who each subscriber actually is, not a single blast for the whole list.

Marketing Automation

The orchestrator underneath it all: it routes work across the modules, runs deterministic brand and channel checks, enforces the mandatory human-approval gate, and publishes approved content into your existing tools. Nothing goes live without a person's yes.
