The problem

Social drifts furthest from the rest of the brand

  • Different team, different voice. Social is often staffed separately and briefed loosely, so it ends up describing a different company than your site and email do.
  • Format pressure beats message discipline. Platform conventions change constantly, and chasing them tends to crowd out any consistent point of view.
  • Engagement is not the goal, but it's what gets measured. Likes and follows are immediate and visible; the revenue connection is downstream and usually unattributed.
  • Paid and organic social rarely align. They target different audiences with different messages, often within the same week.

Social — Paid & Organic FAQ

Common questions

Does Triangul8 post to social automatically?

No. Approved copy and creative briefs are delivered as drafts into your workflow. Scheduling and publishing stay with your team — human approval is mandatory at every tier.

Does it create images or video?

It generates copy and creative direction — hooks, angles, captions, and briefs that tell a designer or video editor what to make and why. Asset production stays with your creative team or tools.

How is paid social different from organic here?

They're separate channels. Organic social is scored on engagement quality and persona affinity; paid social is scored on acquisition cost. Both read from the same personas, which is what keeps the two consistent.

Which platforms are supported?

Meta and TikTok are directly integrated for performance data and content delivery. Other platforms are supported through flat-file workflows, and the copy itself is generated for whichever format you specify.

Can it match our existing brand voice on social?

Yes. Brand voice is loaded once and enforced on every draft, including the looser register social usually calls for. The tone can differ by channel while the underlying positioning stays consistent.

Is social worth running if we're already doing SEO and email?

It depends on whether your buyers discover through feed or through search. For brand-led and lifestyle categories, social often drives the first touch that search later captures. The combinations most customers choose are covered on the pricing page.
