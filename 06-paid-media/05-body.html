Paid Media FAQ

Common questions

Which paid channels are supported?

Paid Search, Programmatic, and Social Paid are available as distinct channels, each ingesting its own performance data and receiving generated copy back. Google Ads, Meta Ads, TikTok, and The Trade Desk are directly integrated; others connect by API or flat file.

Does it manage bids or budget?

No. Triangul8 handles the creative and copy layer — what the ad says and who it's written for. Bidding, budget allocation, and campaign structure stay in your platforms and with your team.

How does scoring on CPA work before anything runs?

Drafts are scored against the acquisition economics of the persona being targeted, using historical performance from that channel. It's a pre-flight check against what has converted for comparable segments, not a prediction of exact cost.

Can it keep landing pages consistent with ads?

That's one of the main reasons to run paid alongside the website content channel. Both are generated from the same persona, so the ad promise and the page that receives the click describe the same customer in the same language.

Will it replace my paid media specialist?

No — it removes the copy production and brief-writing load so that person spends their time on strategy, structure, and budget. The expertise that matters in paid is allocation and testing discipline, not writing variant number nine.

Does anything go live automatically?

No. Ad copy and creative are delivered as drafts or paused items. Human approval is mandatory and can't be disabled.
