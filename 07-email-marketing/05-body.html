The problem

Your list is segmented by lifecycle, not by behavior

  • Stage is not a segment. "New subscriber" and "lapsed" describe where someone sits in a funnel, not what actually persuades them. Two people at the same stage can need opposite messages.
  • Open rate flatters. A high open rate on a send that produced no revenue looks like success on a dashboard and like nothing at all on the P&L.
  • Behavioral segmentation is too manual to maintain. Building and refreshing segments by hand across a growing list is why most teams default to lifecycle stage and leave the real signal unused.
  • Flows go stale quietly. Lifecycle sequences get written once and run for years while the customer base underneath them shifts.

Email FAQ

Common questions

How is this different from the segmentation in my ESP?

Most ESP segmentation is rule-based: you define the conditions and maintain them. Triangul8 derives segments from behavioral clustering across your full customer data, including sources outside the ESP, and refreshes them automatically. The practical difference is that you discover segments you wouldn't have thought to define.

What is revenue per send and why score on it?

Revenue per send divides attributable revenue by emails delivered. It's the metric that accounts for both engagement and monetization in one number, which is why it survives scrutiny in a way open rate doesn't. A send with a lower open rate and higher revenue per send is the better send.

Does it send emails automatically?

No. Approved content publishes into your ESP as drafts or paused campaigns. A person always makes the final call to deploy — there's no setting that removes the approval gate.

Will it work if my list is small?

Clustering needs enough customers to find statistically meaningful groups — roughly 250 matched customers per segment. Smaller lists still work, with broader segments. The content quality improves either way; the segmentation granularity is what scales with list size.

Can it write for flows as well as campaigns?

Yes. Automated sequences — welcome, abandonment, replenishment, win-back, post-purchase — are often where persona targeting produces the clearest lift, because they run continuously against a defined behavioral trigger.

Which email platforms are supported?

Klaviyo is directly supported as a bidirectional integration: engagement data flows in to sharpen personas, and approved campaigns flow back out as drafts. Other platforms connect by API or flat file.
