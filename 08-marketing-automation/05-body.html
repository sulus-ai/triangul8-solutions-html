Marketing Automation

The orchestrator, the brand checks, the approval gate, and direct publishing -- to every channel.

The problem

Your automation tool can schedule and send. It has no opinion on who a piece of content is for, whether it's on-brand, or whether it's ready to go live -- so "automation" quietly becomes "unreviewed publishing" the moment volume goes up.

Most marketing automation platforms automate the mechanics -- send this, post that, on this schedule -- without ever touching whether the content itself is right for the person receiving it. That works fine until volume scales past what a human can manually spot-check, at which point automation starts shipping things nobody actually reviewed.

What Triangul8 does

Marketing Automation is the orchestrator underneath every other module: it routes work, runs deterministic brand and channel checks (banned terms, mandatory claims, reading level, format rules) as a pass-or-fail gate, and holds everything at a human approval step before anything ships -- in both manual Build mode and automated Auto mode. Approved content then publishes directly into your ad platform, ESP, CMS, or seller account as a draft, never live without you.

Marketing Automation FAQ

Common questions

Is this a replacement for my marketing automation platform?

Not usually. Platforms like HubSpot or Klaviyo handle campaign execution, scheduling, and customer journeys. Triangul8 handles the layer upstream of that — producing the content, governing it, and delivering approved drafts into those platforms. Most customers run both.

What are deterministic gates?

Checks with a binary outcome: a banned term is present or it isn't, a mandatory claim appears or it doesn't, reading level clears the threshold or it fails. They run on every draft before human review, so your reviewers spend time on judgment rather than catching rule violations.

Can we customize the brand rules?

Yes. Voice, banned terms, required disclosures, reading level, and channel-specific legal requirements are configured during implementation and can be updated any time. At Enterprise, rules can differ per brand or market.

Who approves content, and can it be skipped?

Your team approves, and it cannot be skipped. Mandatory human approval is included at every tier and there is no setting that removes it. Approval routing can be configured to match your existing review structure.

Does this handle scheduling and send times?

No. Scheduling, journey logic, and send-time optimization stay in your marketing automation platform. Triangul8 delivers approved content there as drafts.

What happens to performance data after publishing?

It flows back as an input to persona development. Opens, clicks, and conversions sharpen the segments that produced the content, which is what makes the next cycle better than the last.
